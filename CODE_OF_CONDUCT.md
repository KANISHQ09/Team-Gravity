# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We, the contributors and maintainers of GRAVITY, pledge to make taking part in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

GRAVITY is an educational project whose audience includes students and young people. We hold ourselves to a standard that keeps the project welcoming and safe for them.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind, patient and respectful, especially to beginners.
- Give and receive constructive feedback gracefully; critique ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to anyone affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the team and the people who will use GRAVITY.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or private.
- Sexualised language or imagery of any kind.
- Personal or political attacks, trolling, or deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without explicit permission.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate around young learners.
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces (the repository, issues, pull requests, reviews, team chats and calls, and hackathon events) and when someone officially represents the project.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, contact a project maintainer privately (for example by direct message to a maintainer listed in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS)). If the concern involves a maintainer, contact a different maintainer. For events, you may also contact the NASA Space Apps local organisers.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential as far as possible. Reporters will not face retaliation.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, for example:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning explaining why the behaviour was inappropriate.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban:** a temporary ban from interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban:** a permanent ban from the project community.
